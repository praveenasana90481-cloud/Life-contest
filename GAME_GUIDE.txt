THE LIFE QUEST — GAME EDITION

Open index.html to start.

FLOW
1. Player enters a name and selects an avatar.
2. A cinematic introduction begins.
3. Level 1 teaches the beginning of Planet Earth.
4. Reading all story scenes unlocks the mini-game.
5. Completing the mini-game unlocks the five-question quiz.
6. A score of 3/5 or higher completes the level and unlocks the next chapter.
7. Progress, XP, coins, stars, badges and best scores are stored in the browser using localStorage.

CONTENT
- 20 sequential story-game levels
- Original 60-page knowledge library retained as library.html
- Every game page is self-contained with inline CSS and JavaScript
- No internet or external library is required

RESET
Use “Change player” on the home screen to erase game progress and begin again.
