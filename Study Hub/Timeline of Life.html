Timeline of Life

A visual journey from Earth’s formation to modern humans.

📖 Interactive lesson💾 Progress saved locally📱 Responsive design

Deep-time timeline

4.6 billion years ago

Earth forms from material around the young Sun.

4.4–4.0 billion years ago

Crust and persistent liquid-water environments develop.

At least 3.5 billion years ago

Clear evidence of microbial life is present.

About 2.4 billion years ago

The Great Oxidation Event raises environmental oxygen.

About 1.8 billion years ago

Early eukaryotic cells are present.

About 600 million years ago

Complex multicellular animals diversify.

541 million years ago

Cambrian diversification appears prominently in fossils.

About 470 million years ago

Plants colonise land.

About 370 million years ago

Early tetrapods enter shallow-water and land environments.

66 million years ago

Non-avian dinosaurs disappear; mammals diversify afterward.

About 300,000 years ago

Early Homo sapiens are present in Africa.

Present

Life occupies nearly every major environment and continues to evolve.

Evidence note: Dates are approximate and may be revised as new rock, fossil and molecular evidence is analysed.
