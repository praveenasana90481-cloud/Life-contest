Why Classify?

Classification gives every organism an organised place. It helps scientists identify organisms, compare features and communicate without confusion.

2

Domains of Life

Modern classification recognises three domains: Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya. Bacteria and Archaea are prokaryotic; Eukarya contains organisms with eukaryotic cells.

3

Kingdom Patterns

Organisms may be compared by cell type, number of cells, nutrition, reproduction and evolutionary relationships.

4

Scientific Names

Binomial nomenclature gives each species a two-part name: genus followed by species, such as Homo sapiens.

🧠 Key facts

  • Taxonomy is the science of naming and classifying organisms.
  • Phylogeny describes evolutionary relationships.
  • A scientific species name has a capitalised genus and lower-case species.
